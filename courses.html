Bachelor of Theology (B.Th.)

A comprehensive undergraduate program providing foundational knowledge in Biblical Studies, Systematic Theology, Church History and Practical Ministry.

3 Years
Full-Time / Blended

Core Curriculum

  • Old Testament Survey & Pentateuchal Studies
  • New Testament Survey & Gospels Architecture
  • Introduction to Systematic Theology I & II
  • Spiritual Disciplines & Character Formation
  • Introduction to Biblical Greek
  • Systematic Theology III
  • General Pastoral Care & Counseling
  • Cross-Cultural Missions & Church Planting
  • Senior Theological Research Thesis (10,000 words)
  • Supervised Field Ministry Practicum
