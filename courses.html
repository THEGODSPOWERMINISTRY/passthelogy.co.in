Our Academic Programs

Biblical education, theological formation and practical ministry preparation.

Choose Your Course

Certificate Program

Certificate in Theology (C.Th.)

An accessible introductory theological program designed for believers, Sunday school teachers and marketplace professionals.

1 Academic Year
Part-Time / Weekend

Program Overview

The Certificate in Theology (C.Th.) is an introductory program designed to build solid theological understanding alongside regular vocational and ministry responsibilities.

Core Curriculum

  • How We Got the Bible: Canonization & Authority
  • Summary Analysis of the Old and New Testaments
  • Core Tenets of the Christian Faith
  • Workplace Ministry & Marketplace Witness Integration
Diploma Program

Diploma in Theology (D.Th.)

A practical theological program for church workers, evangelists, youth leaders and marketplace ministries.

2 Academic Years
Full-Time / Modular

Program Overview

The Diploma in Theology (D.Th.) provides a streamlined but thorough biblical education focused on practical ministry and direct leadership deployment.

Core Curriculum

  • Comprehensive Old & New Testament Overviews
  • Basic Christian Doctrine & Faith Fundamentals
  • Methods of Personal Evangelism & Discipleship
  • Children and Youth Ministry & Church Administration
Undergraduate Degree

Bachelor of Theology (B.Th.)

A comprehensive undergraduate program providing foundational knowledge in Biblical Studies, Systematic Theology, Church History and Practical Ministry.

3 Years
Full-Time / Blended

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Theology (B.Th.) provides an extensive undergraduate foundation in biblical analysis, historical doctrine and practical ministry leadership.

Core Curriculum

  • Old Testament Survey & Pentateuchal Studies
  • New Testament Survey & Gospels Architecture
  • Introduction to Systematic Theology I & II
  • Spiritual Disciplines & Character Formation
  • Introduction to Biblical Greek
  • Systematic Theology III
  • General Pastoral Care & Counseling
  • Cross-Cultural Missions & Church Planting
  • Senior Theological Research Thesis
  • Supervised Field Ministry Practicum

Postgraduate Degree

Master of Divinity (M.Div.)

Designed to equip leaders for professional Christian ministry with advanced biblical studies, theological training and pastoral leadership development.

2 – 3 Years
Full-Time / Modular

Program Overview

The Master of Divinity (M.Div.) is designed for professional pastoral leadership, chaplaincy and advanced Christian teaching ministries. The program provides deeper theological study, biblical language training and ministry development.

Core Curriculum

  • Advanced Biblical Hebrew & Greek Exegesis
  • Advanced Hermeneutics & Contemporary Theological Methods
  • Exegetical Studies in the Prophets & Pauline Epistles
  • Apologetics & Applied Christian Ethics
  • Pastoral Leadership & Ministry Development
  • Advanced Biblical & Theological Research
  • Master's Graduate Defense Paper
