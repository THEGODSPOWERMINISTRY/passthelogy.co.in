Our Academic Programs

Explore theological programs designed to equip faithful servants for biblical knowledge, spiritual formation, pastoral ministry, missions and Christian leadership.

Choose Your Program

PASS Theological Seminary offers four academic pathways — Certificate, Diploma, Bachelor and Master level theological education. Select a program below to view its duration, instructional mode, purpose and core curriculum.

B.TH.

Bachelor of Theology

A well-rounded undergraduate theological foundation in biblical analysis, historical doctrine and practical leadership, preparing students for pastoral ministry, missions and theological research.

Level Undergraduate Degree
Duration 3 Academic Years
Mode Full-Time / Blended
Focus Ministry & Theology

Core Curriculum

  • Old Testament Survey & Pentateuchal Studies
  • New Testament Survey & Gospels Architecture
  • Introduction to Systematic Theology I & II
  • Spiritual Disciplines & Character Formation
  • Introduction to Biblical Greek
  • Systematic Theology III
  • General Pastoral Care & Counseling
  • Cross-Cultural Missions & Church Planting
  • Senior Theological Research Thesis (10,000 words)
  • Supervised Field Ministry Practicum
View Prospectus Application Form
M.DIV.

Master of Divinity

A postgraduate professional program designed for pastoral leadership, chaplaincy and academic teaching ministries, with deeper biblical languages, theological studies and ministerial training.

Level Postgraduate Degree
Duration 3 Years / Advanced Tracks
Mode Full-Time / Modular
Focus Pastoral & Academic

Core Curriculum

  • Advanced Biblical Hebrew & Greek Exegesis
  • Advanced Hermeneutics & Contemporary Theological Methods
  • Exegetical Studies in the Prophets & Pauline Epistles
  • Apologetics: Defending Christian Orthodoxy & Applied Christian Ethics
  • Master's Graduate Defense Paper (15,000 words)
View Prospectus Application Form
D.TH.

Diploma in Theology

A streamlined but thorough biblical education designed for church lay leaders, evangelists, youth directors and marketplace ministries, with emphasis on practical biblical literacy and leadership.

Level Undergraduate Diploma
Duration 2 Academic Years
Mode Full-Time / Modular
Focus Biblical & Practical

Core Curriculum

  • Comprehensive Old & New Testament Overviews
  • Basic Christian Doctrine & Faith Fundamentals
  • Methods of Personal Evangelism & Discipleship Track
  • Basics of Children and Youth Ministry Architecture & Church Administration
View Prospectus Application Form
C.TH.

Certificate in Theology

An accessible introductory theology program designed for marketplace professionals, Sunday school instructors and believers who desire stronger theological foundations alongside their vocational careers.

Level Certificate Program
Duration 1 Academic Year
Mode Part-Time / Weekend
Focus Foundational Theology

Core Curriculum

  • How We Got the Bible: Canonization & Authority
  • Summary Analysis of the Old and New Testaments
  • Core Tenets of the Christian Faith (The Creedal Framework)
  • Workplace Ministry & Marketplace Witness Integration
